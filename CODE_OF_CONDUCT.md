# Code of Conduct

Lume Portal is a small community project. Everyone who participates —
issues, pull requests, discussions — is expected to:

- Be respectful and constructive. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Assume good faith, and keep criticism specific and actionable.
- Not harass, insult, discriminate, or publish anyone's private
  information.
- Not submit code, content, or credentials you do not have the right to
  share (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)).

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when behavior
breaks these rules. Decisions are made in the open when possible; if you
believe a decision was wrong, open an issue and say so calmly.

This is a short, practical version of the usual open-source conduct rules,
not a legal document. The goal is simple: keep the project a place people
want to contribute to.
